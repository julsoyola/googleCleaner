Privacy Policy

This application is a private, personal-use tool used only by its owner to manage and organize files in their own Google Drive account via rclone.

It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, files, or account information belonging to any user, and it has no users other than its owner.

Any data accessed through the Google Drive API is used exclusively by the owner for local file management purposes and is never sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties.

For questions about this policy, contact the repository owner via GitHub.
