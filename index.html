GooSweep

GooSweep is a Chrome extension for reviewing files and folders you own in Google Drive. Search by name, filter by file type and modification date, sort results, and review your selection before confirming Move to Trash.

GooSweep requests Google Drive access to list your existing items and their metadata, identify the signed-in account, and move the items you select to Trash. Moving a folder to Trash also moves its contents. The extension does not permanently delete files or empty Trash.

Cleanup runs in the background, with progress and results stored locally on your device. Document contents are not downloaded or read.
